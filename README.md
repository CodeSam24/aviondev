# Sam's Web Studio

Build me a modern, premium freelancing website for my web-development business.

The website is NOT a personal developer portfolio. It is a client-facing website designed to turn visitors into potential clients who want a website built or redesigned.

BUSINESS:

I am Sam, a young freelance web developer who builds modern websites for small businesses, creators, coaches, and local businesses.

MAIN GOAL:

When someone visits the site, they should immediately understand:

1. What I do

2. Who I help

3. What kind of websites I build

4. Why they should trust me

5. How to contact me

DESIGN:

- Dark, minimalist, premium aesthetic

- Black / white / dark-gray color palette

- Subtle accent color only where necessary

- Modern typography

- Lots of whitespace

- Clean spacing and strong visual hierarchy

- Smooth but subtle animations

- Professional rather than flashy

- Fully responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile

- Make it feel like a legitimate modern web-development agency

- Avoid generic AI-generated website aesthetics

- Do NOT use stock photos of businessmen, handshakes, offices, etc.

- Do NOT make the design overly complicated

NAVIGATION:

Home

Services

Work

Process

About

Contact

HERO SECTION:

Headline:

"Modern websites for businesses that want to stand out."

Supporting text:

"I design and build fast, responsive websites tailored to your business, brand, and customers."

Primary CTA:

"Start a Project"

Secondary CTA:

"View My Work"

SERVICES SECTION:

Create four service cards:

1. Business Websites

"Professional websites designed to give your business a strong online presence."

2. Landing Pages

"Focused, high-converting pages built around a specific product, service, or goal."

3. Website Redesigns

"Transform outdated websites into modern, responsive experiences."

4. Website Maintenance

"Keep your website updated, polished, and running smoothly after launch."

WORK SECTION:

Create a visually impressive project showcase.

Use real projects only. Do not invent clients, testimonials, statistics, or experience.

Include placeholder project cards for:

- Burger Dev

- Progressive RPM

- Coach Website

- Freelancing Website

Each project should have:

- Large project preview/image area

- Project name

- Short description

- Technologies used

- "View Project" button

Make the project showcase feel like a premium case-study section rather than a generic gallery.

WHY WORK WITH ME:

Create four concise benefits:

"Custom Built"

"Websites designed around your business instead of forcing your business into a template."

"Responsive"

"Designed to look and work properly across phones, tablets, and desktops."

"Direct Communication"

"Work directly with the person designing and building your website."

"Modern Design"

"Clean, professional interfaces built to make your business look credible online."

PROCESS SECTION:

Create a four-step process:

01 — Tell Me What You Need

"Share your business, goals, style, and what you want your website to accomplish."

02 — Design & Build

"I design and develop the website around your brand and requirements."

03 — Review

"You review the website and provide feedback for revisions."

04 — Launch

"Once everything is ready, your website goes live."

PRICING SECTION:

Create three pricing tiers.

Starter

"Simple landing pages and smaller websites."

"Starting at $XXX"

Business

"Multi-page websites for businesses and organizations."

"Starting at $XXX"

Custom

"Larger or specialized projects with custom requirements."

"Let's Talk"

Clearly label prices as starting prices so they can be changed later.

ABOUT SECTION:

Headline:

"Built by a developer who actually cares about the details."

Text:

"I'm Sam, a freelance web developer focused on building modern websites for businesses and creators. I started learning web development because I wanted to understand how websites actually work—not just use them. Now I use those skills to help businesses build a stronger online presence."

Keep this section concise and authentic. Do not exaggerate my experience or claim years of professional experience that I do not have.

CONTACT SECTION:

Headline:

"Have a project in mind?"

Supporting text:

"Tell me what you're looking to build, and I'll get back to you."

Create a professional project inquiry form with:

- Name

- Email

- Business / Company

- Project type

- Budget

- Message

Primary button:

"Send Project Inquiry"

Also display my email as an alternative contact method.

FOOTER:

Include:

- Business name / Sam

- Short description

- Navigation links

- Social links placeholders

- Email

- Copyright

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

- Use clean, modular React/TypeScript code.

- Use reusable components.

- Make the site fully responsive.

- Optimize performance.

- Use accessible semantic HTML.

- Add proper hover/focus states.

- Add smooth scrolling.

- Add subtle page/section animations without hurting performance.

- Make buttons and forms functional.

- Use SEO-friendly metadata.

- Make the project cards easy to update later.

- Keep all business information, pricing, project information, and contact information easy to modify.

IMPORTANT:

Do not invent clients, reviews, awards, revenue numbers, customer counts, years of experience, or other credentials.

The finished website should feel like a real freelance web-development business, not an AI-generated template.
